# Cross-category framework

| Food/beverage device | Beauty/personal-care translation | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “一口就有感觉” | “上脸/上手第一感受” | Describe texture, spread, finish, scent, or comfort. |
| Ingredient origin/process | Formula story, active, texture technology | Use only substantiated facts. |
| Solves thirst/craving | Solves a routine pain point: dryness, stickiness, dull look, tangled hair, odor | Name a specific person and occasion. |
| Refreshing/satisfying payoff | Clean, hydrated, smooth, soft, lightweight, polished feeling | Prefer observable sensations over guarantees. |
| Serving suggestion | Layering, order of use, amount, frequency, touch-up | Follow the product label. |
| “囤货/复购” | Easy daily ritual or travel convenience | Avoid fabricated scarcity. |

## Beat patterns

**Tension -> sensory proof -> routine**: Hook with a recognizable moment; show texture/use in close-up; explain one reason it fits; show a qualified feeling or appearance payoff; invite saving or commenting.

**Counterintuitive comparison**: Challenge a common shortcut; compare use situations or textures; demonstrate visibly; recommend only for the matching scenario.

**场景菜单 montage**: Use three short scenes (commute, desk, night routine), one benefit-feeling per scene, then summarize the routine.

## Language bank

Prefer: “摸起来”, “推开后”, “洗完不拔干的感觉”, “薄涂更轻”, “适合空调房/通勤/熬夜后”, “帮助维持”, “按产品说明使用”.

Avoid without evidence: “根治”, “零刺激”, “百分百”, “立刻逆龄”, “药妆级”, “替代治疗”, “全网第一”, “永久不脱”, and unqualified “敏感肌都能用”.
